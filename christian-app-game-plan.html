Game Plan

Build one Bible verse memorization app on public-domain text, ship it to iPhone and Android from Windows, test it on your own kids and classes, then sell church licenses. Games reuse the same code. PC/Steam comes last.

Use BSB + KJV

Public domain, free for a paid app. NIV isn't licensable; ESV is orgs-only; NLT/CSB cost $29/mo + $10/mo each via API.Bible. YouVersion's API is non-commercial only.

Build with Expo

One codebase for iPhone and Android. EAS Build compiles iOS on Expo's Macs — no Mac needed. Free tier: 15 iOS + 15 Android builds a month.

No ads, no trackers

Kids apps can't send personal or device data to third parties. That rules out RevenueCat too — use the stores' own billing directly.

Parental gate everywhere

Before any purchase, outbound link, or permission request. Build it first so it's never a rejection.

Support iPad from day one

Sunday school rooms run on iPads. Plan iPad layouts and screenshots up front.

The LLC is a $400/yr decision, not a requirement

You can launch as an individual for $124 total and form the LLC later, once the church plan proves itself. Until then your legal name shows as the seller on Apple. Apple converts individual accounts to organization on request.

Competitors (checked Sept 2026)

The category is not empty. Bible Memory App Pro: $9.99/mo, $49.99/yr, $149.99 lifetime. Fighter Verses: $4.99 + $0.99–2.99 translation add-ons. Remember Me and Versify: free, no ads. Type the Word: per-student school licenses with Google Classroom sync. Bible Memory Kids exists.

So: your edge is the church/class layer and simplicity for young kids, not the memorization mechanic. Cap the consumer unlock at $4.99–6.99. The money is the church plan.

Verification log — what was checked Sept 12, 2026
  • Tennessee LLC: $300 to form; $300 annual report due April 1 (exempt in formation year); franchise tax $100 min; excise 6.5% after $50k deduction. SaaS is taxable in TN; app-store sales are collected by Apple/Google.
  • Google Play: org accounts need D-U-N-S and are outside the 12-testers/14-day rule (personal accounts created after Nov 13, 2023, per app). Updates never need testers.
  • Screenshots: Apple 6.9" iPhone set + 13" iPad set, auto-scaled down; Google 2–8 phone shots at 1080×1920 + 1024×500 feature graphic.
  • Apple 3.1.3: enterprise carve-out only for apps sold only to orgs. A consumer app unlocking paid content by code is rejected under 3.1.1. Use 3.1.3(b): the church plan must also exist as an IAP.
  • RevenueCat: forces a Purchase History privacy disclosure and has caused repeated Kids-category rejections. Replaced with direct store billing (react-native-iap).
  • Kids category: parental gate before links, purchases, permissions; no personal/device data to third parties; age bands 5-and-under, 6–8, 9–11. Guideline 5.1.4 applies the gate to any app primarily for under-13s.
  • YouVersion Platform: non-commercial only; paywalls or paid tiers revoke access.
  • Reviewer-dependent, can't pre-verify: whether a given reviewer accepts your parental gate and 3.1.3(b) setup first time. Write clear review notes; expect one round.
