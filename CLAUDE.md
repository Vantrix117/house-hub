# House Hub — instructions for Claude

The Anderson family's one-stop app, used on iPad, iPhone and desktop. This file is the map; read it before touching anything.

## Where things run

| Piece | Where | How it deploys |
|---|---|---|
| Site (`index.html`, `apps/`, `sw.js`, `offline.html`, `icons/`) | **GitHub Pages**, `main` branch, repo root → https://vantrix117.github.io/house-hub/ | `git push` — Pages rebuilds in 30–90 s. Always confirm the live file returns 200 before saying it is live. |
| API + database | **Cloudflare Worker `house-hub-api`** + **D1 `house-hub`** → https://house-hub-api.catalystfarm1.workers.dev | `cd worker && npx wrangler deploy`. Editing `worker/` changes nothing until you deploy. Full reference: [`worker/README.md`](worker/README.md). |
| Secrets | Cloudflare Worker secrets: `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` | `npx wrangler secret put NAME` from `worker/` — it prompts. **Never write a secret, a PIN or the pairing code into this repo, a `.dev.vars` you commit, or a chat transcript.** |

There is no build step, bundler, framework or `package.json` anywhere. Do not add one. The Worker is plain ESM that wrangler bundles; it has no npm dependencies.

## How the hub works

- **Pairing + profiles.** A device is paired once with the household pairing code (hash in D1 `settings`; wrong codes are limited per device install id `hub.fp` and, looser, per IP). Then each person picks their profile: kids and the kiosk open on tap, adults create a 4–8 digit PIN on first tap and use it afterwards (Me → Change my PIN, `POST /api/me/pin`). After an admin **Reset PIN** the person first types the **one-time 6-digit code** the admin was shown (24 h), then chooses a new PIN — no other device can claim the account meanwhile; resetting an admin (your own or a co-admin's) needs the resetting admin's PIN. Wrong PINs and codes are counted before they are checked and limited per device (5 per 15 min, 20 a day) and per profile across all devices (10 an hour, then a pause of 1 h doubling to 24 h, and a push to the admin); a device the person has signed in on before is never held by that pause. The admin's own row offers Change PIN, not a reset. Tokens live in `localStorage` (`hub.device`, `hub.session`) and are shared by every app on the origin.
- **The Kitchen device** (KITCHEN-1, `migrations/006-kitchen.sql`). The admin marks one paired device as the kitchen from another device: Me → Admin → Devices → "Make it the kitchen" / "Not the kitchen", confirmed with the admin's PIN (`PUT /api/admin/devices/:id/role {role, admin_pin}`; the kitchen itself has no way into admin). It then signs in only as the `kitchen` profile (kind `kitchen`, no PIN; the shell asks `GET /api/device` at boot and after any 401, so it never shows the picker), and no other device can sign in as the kitchen. It writes the family apps (Larder, family prayer list, reminders; its own Timer and Tally rows) and nothing personal: no private lists, F260, Verses, chat, push or admin. When it credits someone (a name in `prayedBy`, the Larder's `finishedBy`, a feed line via `hub.activity(text, app, { as })`, an album photo `as`) that must be a household member, never a kid on the Larder. The kitchen is not a person: pickers and `hub.people()` leave it out, and the admin can change only its colour. **Its shell** (KITCHEN-2, batch 2a): no picker (offline, a device that was the kitchen waits on "Waiting for the house" instead), no Me or Chat tab (`#me`/`#chat` land on Home), the Apps list is the Larder, Prayer (the family list only), Timer and Tally, and Home is `renderKitchenHome()` in `index.html`: the clock, the kitchen's running timer, food to eat soon, today's family prayers with the faces of who prayed, reminders (add; clear with Undo, removed for everyone after 6 s like Home's) and the family album with Add, in glance-size type (`--fs-glance-*`), repainted in place. **Who gets credit**: `hub.whoDidThis({ who: 'household' | 'adults', title, message })` → the tapped person or null — a sheet of the household's faces, no PIN (attribution, not a sign-in; never guests, the TV or the kitchen). Prayer's Prayed (and a prayer-mode run, asked once) shows everyone, kids included, and ticks that person's id; the Larder's ✓ and the album's Add show the adults only (P5-D2) and send `finishedBy` / `{as}`. The feed line goes with `{ as }`. P2-PROF-09 (the shared iPad never signing out) is closed by this: nobody signs in as a person there, so there is nothing to time out.
- **Kids** (Ezra, Kiara): kid mode shows only apps whose `visibleTo` lists them — Kid Verse (`apps/kidverse.html`: the week's F260 memory-verse refs with a clearly-labelled paraphrase, story art, read-aloud, one ★ per day; stars as one row per star in the kid's person scope (batch 0f), derived and mirrored to family `stars:<kid>` so adults and the TV read them; an adult sets the family week from the same app), the family prayer list only (big cards: "Pray" with drawn hands, then their face, a ✓ and "Prayed" in a clearly different colour; the request read aloud on tap; faces, no inputs), Larder (a read-only picture view: food pictures, big names, freshness colour, no ✓ or add bar), Tally, Timer, the park map.
- **TV** (kiosk Home, re-laid out in audit batch 2c): an ambient board that is always exactly one screen — album photos blurred with a slow crossfade (ambient art when the album is empty) behind solid panes: the clock, the week's verse with Kid Verse's paraphrase for that week (labelled a paraphrase; a copy of Kid Verse's table, checked by `test-tv.mjs`), who prayed today, who read today (household adults only, no guests; ✓ from `GET /api/f260/readers`, the F260 logs, asked once a minute and kept on the device in `hub.tv.readers` for an offline reopen; when the kept answer is not today's — a Worker without the route, a house that cannot be reached — today's "Read week…" feed lines instead), the kids' stars, the last five feed lines and the reminders, newest first, each whole on up to two lines, when there are any. **Answered-prayer anniversaries** (IMP-PRAYER-I2, batch 3): on a day when a request on the family list was answered on this month and day in an earlier year (its `answeredAt`, the household's New York date; a 29 February answer on the 28th in other years), the board adds one quiet line, "A year ago today · Answered: <title>" (the most recent year, then the title in order): in the verse pane when showing it leaves row 1 as tall as it was (`placeAnniv()`, the faces' name size; it fits there at 1920 × 1080 with a one-line title), otherwise first in the feed, where it may take the place of the oldest lines but never of the last one, else not at all — always whole, never clipped. The TV reads only the family prayer rows, so a private title can never reach it. A 12-column grid in three rows (clock + verse · today · feed + reminders, half and half); after each paint `fit()` keeps each pane's faces in one row with a "+N" face (every face shown first, who prayed and who read each keeping room for their own first labelled face and its "+N", who prayed fitted last, so a rotation never keeps an old fit) and shows the feed lines and reminders that fit, the rest of the reminders counted in the heading ("Reminders · +3 more"; a list with no room for one row counts itself there too), so nothing runs off the screen. hub.js sets `data-tv-scale="10ft"` on the display, so a 1600 px+ screen gets the 10-foot scale (design.css §7: 28 px floor, ×1.5 spacing, the title-safe margins; decision D16; a 4K screen at 1× — 3840 × 2160 CSS px — stays at those 1920 sizes, as design.css's width tiers stop at 1920); below 1600 px the kiosk's own sizes, with a tighter block under 600 px of height (a TV browser that reports 960 × 540); portrait (a kiosk iPad) gives the feed the full width. No inputs: a hash change to Me, Chat, Apps or an app is refused and the display's Me renders nothing. The board's one control, Switch, opens a two-choice sheet: **Switch profile** stops the board (`stopBoard`, also whenever the picker shows: the tick stops, the wake lock is released, the kept readers are forgotten), ends the session and opens the picker; **Screen look** shows the theme cards (four a row on a landscape TV), Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion and solid glass (no text size), which exist only while the sheet is open and stay on the device (no row, no toast). Both sheets work from a remote: focus starts on the first button, the arrow keys move between the buttons, a choice keeps focus on its control, and the Switch does nothing while a sheet is open. The board takes the screen wake lock itself when the page loads, when the display signs in, on every return to view and on a tap or a remote's key, never from a repaint (a one-line "Press any button or tap the screen to keep it on" hint only after two refusals); on any other device the picker never releases the lock a tap took. Timers and nodes are constant over 24 h (`window.__tvStats`, proven by `test-tv.mjs`, which also counts live intervals and resize listeners and gates the 1920 × 1080 fit).
- **The two Dollywood apps are generated files.** `apps/dollywood.html`, `apps/dollywood-live.html` and `apps/dollywood/*` are exported from the map repo next door (`../dollywood-build-project`: `scripts/template.html` → `build_html.py` → `export_hub.py`). Never edit them here — an export overwrites them. Change the template there, rebuild, run its `verify.py`, export, then commit here. The park map also calls the Worker's `GET /api/dollywood/waits` (queue-times.com proxy, 60 s cache) for live ride waits, and **Rally the family** is `POST /api/dollywood/rally {name, x, y, note?}` / `DELETE /api/dollywood/rally` (household adults only, one per adult per minute): the Worker writes the family meeting point `app_data(family, 'dollywood-live', 'meet')` = `{x, y, name, note, by, byName, at}` (a tombstone on DELETE) so every phone's hub.js picks it up on its next pull, logs "Set a meeting point: <name>" on Home and pushes "Meet at <name>" to the other household adults — contract in `worker/README.md`. Family rows the park map keeps in `app_data(family,'dollywood-live',…)`: `loc:<id>` positions, `meet` (the meeting point; also written by `POST /api/dollywood/rally`), `kid:<id>` heights, `kidshare:<id>` kid beacons — a kid's device stays view-only unless an adult has switched that beacon on in the map's Family pane, in which case it may locate and publish its own spot and nothing else.
- **Build guide** (`apps/dollywood.html`) is on hub.js: progress and plot in Eli's person scope, migrated from its old localStorage on first open; theme follows the person like everything else.
- **Guests**: any adult adds a guest from Me → Add a guest (name, an emoji from a grid, colour, optional PIN, expiry tonight / a week / keep) → `POST /api/profiles`; a PIN-less guest opens on tap and can use every adult app and chat; nobody can set a PIN on a guest afterwards (`guest_pin_fixed`); expired guests vanish from the picker, lose their sessions and push subscriptions, and their person-scope data is purged 30 days later (`POST /api/admin/guests/purge` from the cron, or Remove/Purge in the admin panel). Migration `005-guests.sql`.
- **Prayer** (`apps/prayer.html`, audit batch 3; its layout and ids are kept, see Do not touch): two lists, My list (person scope) and the Family list (family scope), each request a `prayer:<id>` row, the days prayed a `prayerDays` row per list. Add names the list it adds to and switches it; Settings names its list and asks (`hub.confirm`) before a first change to the family's; sharing to the family keeps a weekly request's days (an old family copy with no days shows every day, a read-only rule). Unticking takes today off `prayerDays` only when nothing is left marked today (on the Family list only when no one's tick is left), and `recordDays()` also counts each request's `lastPrayedAt` and the family's `prayedBy` days, so no device erases a day another prayed; `repairToday()` puts a missing today back 2 s after a sync has landed, never mid-pull. One Pray now run posts one feed line ("Prayed through N (family) requests", never a My-list title). On the Kitchen device Pray now is **Around the table**: after "Who is praying?" each person at the table taps their face once and every Prayed credits them all through the kitchen's attribution; a personal device credits only its person. Pray now and the Kitchen view go full screen in the hub (`hub.immersive`); the toast is the shared `hub.toast`; every size is a design.css role and every duration a token; icons come from the shared sprite (`icon()` draws `icons/sprite.svg` symbols, audit batch 4), never an emoji or a text glyph. Since batch 4 its own components carry their own names (`.p-chip`, `.p-tag`, `.p-sheet`, `.p-toast`, `.lead`, `.compact`; `.row`, `.list`, `.wrap`, `.dot`, `.mark` stay because `handoff/prayer/check.js` reads them) and its two switches (Mine/Family, Add's list) are design.css's shared segmented control inside a `.ds` row. `audits/tools/phase6/3/prayer-claims-3.mjs <code>` proves the day-less weekly rule, the Kitchen view at midnight, the Sunday wording, the family confirm on Paste, the header on a landscape iPad and Around the table.
- **Memory verses** (`apps/verses.html`, everyone but the kiosk): a Leitner trainer over the person's F260 memorised verses — reference recall, rate Got it / Almost / Not yet, boxes 1–5 with due dates 1/2/4/7/14 days; writes one `recall:<week>-<i>` row per verse (`{box, due, last, streak, s, t}`) in the person's F260 scope, a `rev:<date>:<id>:<device>` count per rating day, and `verses.summary {due, streak, boxes, total, reviewedToday, week, at}` in its own; kids get only the family week's two verses with big buttons.
- **Profiles** (`worker/seed.sql`): Eli (admin), Mae (id `christian`), Elizabeth (Mom), David (Dad), Mea (niece) — adults, Ezra, Kiara (kids), Downstairs TV (kiosk, read-only), Kitchen (id `kitchen`, kind `kitchen`, the shared kitchen device). Each profile has a `hue`, its colour family (`data-accent`): the starting colours are D3 (`migrations/007-profile-hue.sql`, `seed.sql`), a new guest is sky, and a session stored before the hue existed maps its hex through the bootstrap's table. The admin panel (Me tab, admins only) edits name/emoji/colour/kind, resets PINs (showing the one-time code), issues set-up codes, rotates the pairing code, marks the kitchen device, unpairs devices and shows usage. **Colour** (GAP-ACCENT-1): the Edit sheet (and Add a person) offers all 18 families, the people's nine first, then the nine app colours each labelled with its app, and warns — the admin decides — on a colour someone already wears, an app's colour, and one a colour-blind eye would confuse with another person's (`CVD_PAIRS` in `index.html`: the pairs whose `-graphic` rings sit under ΔE2000 6 with protanopia or deuteranopia in either scheme; the Worker accepts only the 18 names). `familyPickerHtml(on, q, people)` + `wireFamilyPicker(root, q, people, onPick)` are the reusable picker. **Household** (GAP-PROF-a2, Me → Admin → Household, each change with the admin's PIN): Add a person (adult or kid; an adult gets a one-time set-up code), Add an admin (a household adult with a PIN of their own), Hand over admin, Take admin away (never the last one), Remove a person (not yourself, an admin, a guest or a shared screen; their own data goes). A person added there sees only the apps open to everyone until `apps.json` `visibleTo` names them.
- **Who may write what** (`worker/src/policy.js`, audit batch 0d). The Worker, not only the UI, enforces the limits: family rows of an app a profile cannot open (`apps.json` `visibleTo`, which the Worker imports) are refused; kids write only their own person rows, their own tick on the family prayer list, their own Kid Verse rows and their own park dot while their beacon is on; only household adults (not guests) step the Kid Verse week, write rewards ledger rows and the park map's kid beacons and heights; the kitchen as above. Chat's tools go through the same rules, and chat takes app visibility from the Worker, never the request. A refused row in a batch comes back with the house's value, which hub.js puts back (toast "That is not something … can change"). Names and emoji with `<` or `>` are refused. A private prayer title never reaches the feed: the Worker refuses a "Prayed for …" / "Answered: …" line that does not name a family-list request ("a private request" is the private wording).
- **Maps are one row per entry** (audit batch 0e): a whole map in one row lets a device with an older copy erase another device's entry under last-write-wins. F260 keeps `done:<week>-<i>`, `mem:<week>-<i>`, `log:<date>`, `recall:<week>-<i>`; Verses `rev:<date>:<id>:<device>`; the build guide `step:<id>`. The old whole-map rows (`f260.done` …, `progress`) stay as a read-only base that the rows override (`false` = off): read with `hub.rowMap(prefix, legacyKey, opts)` (server: `rowMap()` in `worker/src/data.js`). A new app that keeps a map should do the same.
- **The F260 journal vault** is written whole (iv and ciphertext together, from a copy), only while it is still the version this device unlocked (`vid`; the Worker refuses another version unless the write names it as replaced), never over the Worker's 900 KB, and the panel says "Saved" only after the write took. Face ID wraps are per device (`vault.prf[<device id>]`).
- **F260 reading rules** (`apps/f260.html`, audit batch 4; its layout and ids are kept): an untick takes today off the log (`log:<date>`) only when no other reading was ticked today, and gives back a best streak set today; an unread today is pending, never a rest day (F260 `streakInfo`, Home `logStreak`, chat `logStreak` agree: two rest days between readings keep the streak); a week's start date is set by its first tick or "Start week", never by the stepper; a reading missed in an earlier week shows on Today as "Missed: Week N · Day D — …" with Catch up (counted from the first week read); Reset lists what it clears (the Verses review schedule too) and can be undone for 10 s from a one-level snapshot (Undo pulls first and merges by value, never by clock: rows on now stay on, week dates and milestones merge with the older date winning, best takes the higher; while it can be undone every F260 toast carries the Undo); the journal saves an entry only when its text changed. **Two devices** (batch 4 review): an untick on one device must never erase a day another device read, so `repairLog()` (2 s after a pull has landed, and on load) puts `log:<today>` back when the untick is older than a reading still ticked today, and the Worker applies the same rule (`f260UntickOutlived()` / `f260RecentTicks()` in `worker/src/reminders.js`, used by the evening nudge and `GET /api/f260/readers`; chat's untick too). Readings an Undo or a restore writes back do not count as "ticked today": each writes one person row `restored:<date>:<uid>` = `{ids, upTo}` once, and a `done:` row counts as written back only while it is listed and stamped at or before `upTo` (a re-tick is real); the first 8 am / 8 pm cron firing deletes these rows older than yesterday (`pruneRestored()`). The Today card has a Journal action (`#todayJournal`) and, once today is read, "Read ahead". Each person picks the reading nudge's time (Me → Notifications, `push_pref:readAt`, default 8 pm).
- **Data model.** Everything an app stores is a row in `app_data`: `(scope 'person'|'family', profile_id, app_id, key, value JSON, updated_at)`. Person scope is one person's own data; family scope is shared by the house. Last write wins by `updated_at`; deletes are tombstones. Lists are one row per item (`item:<id>`, `prayer:<id>`) so two people never overwrite each other.
- **`apps/hub.js`** is the SDK every app loads. It keeps a per-app, per-scope `localStorage` cache and a write queue per writer (`hub.queue.<app>.<scope>.<profile>`, family scope too: only that person's session ever sends it), applies writes locally at once, flushes in batches of up to 200 when online (every queue of the person on the device, not only the app's own; and on pagehide with keepalive), pulls on open / visibility / reconnect / every 30 s, and reconciles by `updated_at`. `hub.sync.state` is `synced | pending | offline | error`. Switch (`hub.signOut`) keeps the person's token in `hub.retiring` until their queue and feed lines (`hub.aqueue.<profile>`) have gone with it, then logs out, and removes their person-scope caches at once; `hub.reset()` aborts the previous person's requests. "Forget this device" (adults) sends everything first and refuses while anything is unsent, then `POST /api/device/forget`. Two copies (the shell and an app iframe) share one `localStorage`; `storage` events keep them in step.
- **Themes** (`apps/design.css`, v3 tokens since audit batch 1): named palettes — Hearth (default, warm grouped neutrals), Parchment (soft tan reading paper), Frost (cool pale glass), Midnight (warm dark), Forest (deep green, gold ink), Graphite (true black) — plus System (Hearth by day, Midnight at night). Each is one neutral block under `:root[data-theme=…]` (plus `[data-theme-preview=…]`); the nine hue families are per scheme. `data-theme` on `<html>` is always the RESOLVED palette (System resolves; the choice is `data-theme-choice`); only `:root[data-theme=…]` is a palette scope, a `data-theme` anywhere else is data. hub.js and the pre-paint bootstrap also set `data-scheme="light|dark"` and `color-scheme`, so an app with its own dark CSS keys off `[data-scheme="dark"]`, never `prefers-color-scheme`. **The bootstrap** (`audits/tools/phase4/tokens/bootstrap.js`, copied inline into `<head>` of `index.html` and every app right after the viewport meta and before any stylesheet; in Prayer right after its `themeColor` meta) paints the theme, scheme, accent, kind, preferences and the `theme-color` meta before the first paint. `hub.THEMES` lists the palettes; picked in Me (theme cards) or F260's settings; `docs/design.html` previews and contrast-checks every one; `audits/tools/phase4/tokens/contrast.mjs` is the gate. Adding a theme = the steps in `audits/04-design-system.md` → "Adding a theme" (a neutral block, the scheme selector lists, the bootstrap's `S`/`B` maps and hub.js's `SCHEME_OF`/`THEME_BG`, an entry in `hub.THEMES`, the tests' palette maps); swatches carry `data-theme-preview` + `data-scheme` + `data-accent`.
- **Preferences** live in person scope so they follow the person: the theme is `app_data(person, 'hub', 'theme')` (hub.js mirrors it in `localStorage` so the first paint is right; `hub.setTheme()` writes both). Me → Appearance adds the text size, the glass level (Clear · Current · Frosted · Solid, where Solid is Reduce Transparency), Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion: one hub row each (`textSize`, `glass`, `contrast`, `motion`; `transparency` by API), set with `hub.setTextSize/setGlass/setContrast/setMotion/setTransparency`, read with `hub.prefs()`, mirrored in `localStorage` `hub.prefs` for the bootstrap, applied as `data-text-size`, `data-glass`, `data-contrast`, `data-transparency`, `data-motion` on `<html>` (each mirrors its OS media feature unless set). The display and the kitchen keep their theme and preferences on the device. The text size is one setting for every app (decision D17): F260's Normal/Large writes it, an adult's old `f260.big` moves to Large once on their next F260 open (`f260.bigMoved`) and until then hub.js reads it from F260's cache on that device; F260 keeps only autolock in its own person scope. The display profile can never write — `hub.set` throws `read_only` and shows a toast (`hub.kioskNudge()`); apps check `hub.canWrite` before changing their own UI.
- **Liquid glass** (`apps/design.css`): glass is the navigation and control layer only: tab bar, top bar, sheets, floating buttons, pills and toolbars over content. **Every content card is solid** (`--material-solid-bg`; a `.card` that still carries `.glass` paints solid, with no blur). The recipe is tokens: `--glass-bg` / `--glass-bg-strong` (the specular sheen plus the fill), `--glass-filter` (blur 20 px, `saturate(1.8)`), `--glass-shadow`, and per theme `--glass-spec/-edge/-ring/-inner/-pickup`; `--g` stays the component fill hook. The person's colour is `--glass-pickup-layer`, in the tile-end tone, painted in place on every glass surface; only the tab bar and the top bar drift it, on an `::after` layer that hub.js moves by writing `--sheen-x` on those two bars, never on `:root` (`hub.sheenFrom(el)` for a custom scroller; off under Reduce Motion and on the TV). `--glass-look`, `.glass-lite` and `.mat-look` are deprecated. Solid glass (Reduce Transparency) makes every glass opaque; Increase Contrast raises it to 96/98 %.
- **`apps/design.css`** holds the tokens (Dynamic Type roles, spacing, radii, elevation, the palettes and hue families, kid/kiosk scales, preferences) and then the components. The person's family is `data-accent` (one of 18 names), set by hub.js and the pre-paint bootstrap; `--accent` is an alias of `--accent-strong`, and any element carrying `data-accent` (an avatar, a tile) re-derives every `--accent-*` role for its subtree (`hub.avatarHtml` puts the person's own on every face). Text and icons on a hue read `--X-ink`, a solid `--X-strong` with its `--X-on` label, marks `--X-graphic`; the legacy names (`--gold`, `--olive`, `--muted` …) still resolve, as AA inks. One segmented control serves every app (audit batch 4): `.ds .seg` inside a `.ds` area, or `.segmented` anywhere — a visible track, capsule items, one row when they fit and whole-word rows when they do not (add `.seg-grid` with `--seg-cell` for a long list of choices: equal cells, never one item alone across the track); icons inside apps are `svg.sym` from `icons/sprite.svg`, sized `--icon-*` × the person's text size. Component classes (`.btn`, `.card`, `.row`, …) apply only inside an element with class `ds` — put `class="ds"` on `<body>` to use them, or leave it off and keep your own component CSS (F260, Prayer, Larder and the Dollywood pair do that — but every colour in every app is a token; `screens-apps.mjs` fails on a hex).
- **Kid mode** (`<html data-kind="kid">`): bigger targets and type, only apps whose `visibleTo` includes the kid, simplified Home, kid-safe chat. **Kiosk mode**: no PIN, no tab bar, cannot write; Home shows the clock and the family reminders and refreshes itself.
- **Chat** (Chat tab): `POST /api/chat` → Claude (`claude-sonnet-5`) with tools that read and write `app_data` (list/get/set, add_list_item, add_prayer, mark_prayed, answer_prayer, finish_leftover, set_f260_reading, f260_status, where_is_family, and the kids-only read_todays_verse, which the shell speaks aloud); 60 messages per person per New York day, counted only once the model answers (audit batch 2b: a counter row `rate_limits` `chat:<profile>:<date>` taken in one statement and given back when the upstream fails or times out; the history row is written then too). The Worker gives up on an upstream that sends nothing for 45 s (`upstream_timeout`), and while a reply is on its way the composer's Stop (`#chat-stop`) ends the wait and tells the house (`POST /api/chat/stop {rid}`): no model call or tool runs for that message after it; the message is given back only if the Stop came before the model was asked (a Stop after that counts, so a Stop can never buy model calls outside the cap). The tab offers no Retry: it reloads the history, where anything the house had already done shows, and puts the words back in the field only when the house says no tool had started (`{ran: 0}`). The Worker sends a comment line every 15 s while it thinks or runs a tool, so the tab's own 60 s give-up never fires on a slow but live reply. Thinking blocks go back whole (with their signatures) in the tool loop. Me → Chat history → Clear (`DELETE /api/chat/history`; today's count stays); a kid's chat is deleted after 90 days (the 8 am / 8 pm cron). Full table in `worker/README.md`. The Chat tab (audit batch 2a) shows the last 36 h with day separators and times; a message sent offline waits on the device (`hub.chatq.<profile>`) and goes by itself when the device is back online, whatever the tab (only the signed-in person's own queue; one that still cannot go waits again); a failed send offers Retry; a refused or failed write gets a red chip; kids hear every reply and a spoken question sends itself.

- **Stars & badges** (`apps/kidverse.html` "item 20" blocks + the Me tab's "Kids' rewards" card). A kid earns stars **only** for: (1) the kid verse ★ — one per day, awarded in Kid Verse; (2) hearing the week's story — one per day, read from the family `kidverse` row `story:<kid>` that the story companion marks (each new day there is credited once under `stars.credited.story[date]`); (3) praying on the family prayer list — one per day, read from the family `prayer:*` rows' `prayedBy[date]` lists — profile ids since audit batch 0g; a name an older Prayer wrote counts only while no other profile has it (credited once under `stars.credited.prayed[date]`). Kid Verse reconciles (2) and (3) when it opens, after a ★ and on `hub.onChange`, looking back 14 days. Since audit batch 0f what a kid earned is one row per fact in the kid's person scope — `star:<verse|story|prayed>:<date>` `{at}`, `reset:<kind>:<date>` `{by}`, `applied:<ledgerKey>` `{kind, amount, date, by, at}`, `badge:<id>` `'date'`, `heard:<isoWeek>:<date>` — so a kid's two devices add up instead of the newer whole row erasing the other's; `deriveStars()` rebuilds the stars object from them (the balance by replaying stars, cash-ins and resets in time order) over the pre-0f person row `stars` as a read-only base, and writes it to the family mirror `app_data(kidverse, family, 'stars:<kid>')` (the story's `{week, days}` likewise to `story:<kid>`) whenever it differs. The derived object: `{ week, count, days, total, earned, credited, badges, payouts, applied, earnedAt }` — `week`/`count`/`days` are this ISO week (the Home Kids card and the TV board read `.count`; a stale week reads 0), `total` is the balance a parent can cash in, `earned` is all-time and never resets, `earnedAt[source:date]` is the server-clock ms each star was earned (kept 30 days). **Kid Verse, signed in as that kid, is the only writer of these rows and the mirror.** Badges, each stored once as `badges[id] = date` and celebrated with the calm confetti + a toast + a feed line: `first` First star · `week` Full week (7 in one week) · `ten` Ten stars earned · `story` Story lover (5 stories) · `prayer` Prayer warrior (5 prayed days) · `fifty` Fifty stars earned. Parents (Me → Kids' rewards, adults only, the confirm sheet `hub.confirm()` on both): **Cash in** appends `{ date, amount: total, by }` to `payouts[]` and zeroes `total` (badges and `earned` stay); **Reset week** clears this week so far (dots off, this week's verse and credited days marked `'reset'` so nothing is re-earned or re-credited that day, the week's count taken off `total`); its `days[]` lists this ISO week's dates up to and including the reset day, never the days still to come. Neither touches the stars rows (an adult cannot write a kid's person scope, and a parent's write to the mirror would race the kid's next star under last-write-wins): each action is an append-only family row `app_data(kidverse, family, 'ledger:<kid>:<id>')` = `{ kind: 'cashin' | 'reset', date, amount | days[], by, at }`. Kid Verse applies every ledger row exactly once — on open, after each pull and on `hub.onChange` — remembering it in `stars.applied[key]` (cash-in: `total = max(0, total − amount)` and the payout appended; reset: the listed days' verse ★ and credited days are marked `'reset'` (the day stays spent — one ★ a day holds even after a reset), one star off `total` each — but only stars with `earnedAt ≤ at` or no stamp at all, and only days up to the row's `date`); a star the kid earns while the parent's action is in flight adds on top instead of undoing it, for a cash-in and for a reset alike. The shell (Home Kids card, Me, the TV board's stars pane — all through `kidStars()` → `effectiveStars()`) shows the mirror with any ledger rows the kid has not applied yet on top, so a cash-in reads "★0" at once; only the mirror's raw `.count` lags until the kid's device next opens Kid Verse. Guests never see the card (`!isGuest()`). Totals: kids in Kid Verse's "My rewards" card, adults on Home ("Ezra ★3 · 2 badges · N to cash in") and in Me, the TV board's stars pane.
- **Push** (Me tab): Web Push with VAPID; 8 am fridge warnings to household adults, the F260 reading nudge at the time each person picks (`push_pref:readAt`, "HH:MM" on the half hour, unset = 8 pm; batch 4, IMP-F260-F4), Sunday 8 pm "N readings behind", new family prayers → the other household adults at 8 am and 8 pm (each adult is told each request once, whatever else they got that day; a title edit is not a new request), a kid's park-map dot the house has not heard from for 20 min (the Worker's clock, never the phone's) while a household adult's is fresh → household adults (checked every 15 min, each quiet spell once per adult), who prayed today for your family requests (8 pm, kind `prayedfor`), a reminder to pray at the time you pick (`push_pref:prayAt`, kind `praytime`), and on demand "Meet at <name>" when an adult rallies the family from the park map → the other household adults (on the park-day switch, logged as kind `rally`); per-person toggles for each kind, one row each (`app_data(person, hub, 'push_pref:<kind>')` over the old whole `push_prefs` row, which stays a read-only base; Me repaints when another device changes one). Guests get only their own nudges (reading, catch-up, prayer reminders), never the household's (audit batch 2b). A subscription is one person's on one device: Me → Notifications reads it from the house (`GET /api/push/subscribe`); it ends when the person signs out (`POST /api/logout`), when anyone else signs in on that device, when their session runs out there, and on Reset PIN or a new kind; "Send a test notification" goes to this device only. The display, the kitchen and kids cannot subscribe (403), and `pushTo` never sends to them. An `http:` endpoint is accepted only for a local stand-in (127.0.0.1 / localhost). A subscription needs real keys (400 otherwise); one the push service drops or that cannot be encrypted for is deleted, and never stops a job. Only a delivered push counts toward a kind's day. The service worker moves a replaced subscription itself (`pushsubscriptionchange` → `POST /api/push/resubscribe` with this device's token, which the page hands it with the API address; only this device's rows move). A tap goes to the hub's own top-level window even when an app is open in it, or opens a new one as `index.html?to=<profile>#<app>`; a notification meant for someone else (sent before they switched out) opens nothing of theirs and says whose it was. iPhone/iPad only from the Home Screen app. Rules in `worker/README.md` → Reminders.
- **Updates and offline** (`sw.js`, `index.html`, audit batch 2b): the service worker fetches for itself around the browser's HTTP cache (precache `cache: 'reload'`, revalidate `no-cache`), so a VERSION bump never stores the old files under GitHub Pages' `max-age=600`. The shell asks for a new build when it comes back into view and every hour; a new one activates at once and the page reloads itself on the same tab at an idle moment (hidden or 20 s untouched, and nothing that would be lost: nothing typed or a sheet open in the shell or in the open app's frame, no reply on its way, no PIN being typed), then says "The hub is up to date." A first install says nothing. A document the worker has no copy of, offline, gets `offline.html` (tokens, "Not saved on this device yet", Try again). `manifest.json` has `id` (`./index.html`, what existing installs already are), `lang`, `display_override`, shortcuts and maskable + monochrome icons; `icons/apple-touch-icon.png` is opaque; `node scripts/make-art.mjs --pwa-icons` redraws those three from the icon art.

## Adding an app

1. **Write `apps/<id>.html`, self-contained.** All CSS in `<style>`, all JS in `<script>`, no external scripts, no CDN, no fonts fetched over the network (Prayer used to fetch Google Fonts; since audit batch 1 it uses the system fonts like everything else). Must open standalone *and* inside the hub's iframe.
2. **Import the shared pieces:**
   ```html
   <script>/* the pre-paint bootstrap: audits/tools/phase4/tokens/bootstrap.js, pasted exactly, right after the viewport meta */</script>
   <link rel="stylesheet" href="design.css">
   <script src="hub.js" data-app="<id>" data-scope="person"></script>   <!-- person | family | both -->
   <script>
   (async () => {
     await hub.ready();                                 // profile + cached data are ready; pulls in the background
     const n = hub.get('count', { default: 0 });        // hub.set(key, value) · hub.remove(key) · hub.list('item:')
     hub.onChange(render);                              // another device changed something
     hub.activity('Counted to ' + n);                   // shows in the family feed on Home
     hub.migrate([{ from: 'oldLocalStorageKey', to: 'count' }]);   // optional, once per device, adults only
   })();
   </script>
   ```
   Keys: `[A-Za-z0-9_.:-/]`, ≤ 200 chars. For lists use `item:<id>` rows and `hub.list('item:')`. Check `hub.canWrite` (false for the kiosk) before offering edits. `hub.voiceInput(text => …)` gives speech-to-text where the browser supports it. `hub.toast(msg, ms, { action: 'Undo', onAction })` shows a toast with one button; `await hub.confirm(message, { title, ok, cancel, danger })` asks in the shared confirm sheet (true only from its confirm button or Enter; Cancel, Escape and the backdrop give false) and `hub.alert(message, { title })` is its one-button notice — never the browser's `confirm()`/`alert()`/`prompt()`; `hub.onLeave(fn)` runs `fn` just before the SDK's send on pagehide (a write made on the way out goes with it). `hub.immersive(true)` (batch 3, UX-PRAYER-9) asks the hub's viewer to hide its top bar so the app has the whole screen, and `hub.immersive(false)` brings it back; standalone it does nothing and returns `false`. An app that uses it keeps its own visible Close (≥ 44 px) that calls `hub.immersive(false)`, and handles Escape (calling `preventDefault()` when it closes something itself; an Escape it leaves alone brings the bar back). When the shell brings the bar back by itself (Escape in the shell, the viewer closed) the app gets `hub:immersive` on `window` with `detail.on === false`; `hub.isImmersive()` reads the state. `hub.profile` is `{id, name, kind, isAdmin, isGuest, color, hue, emoji}` (`hub.hueOf(p)` gives anyone's colour family).
3. **Register it in `apps.json`** — one entry, valid JSON, no trailing commas:
   ```json
   { "id": "chores", "name": "Chore board", "file": "apps/chores.html", "color": "#5B6FA8", "hue": "lagoon", "icon": "icons/chores.svg", "scope": "family", "tile": "small", "visibleTo": ["eli", "christian"] }
   ```
   `scope` must match `data-scope`. `hue` is the app's colour family (one of the nine app families of decision D5: coral, apricot, honey, pistachio, leaf, seafoam, lagoon, cornflower, orchid; the tile, its Home card head and every icon of the app carry it as `data-accent`; `color` stays for older copies). `tile` is `small` or `wide` (wide tiles can render a widget in `index.html` → `widgetHtml`). `visibleTo` (profile ids) hides the app from everyone else — omit it for all. Guest profiles (Me → Add a guest) are never listed: a guest sees every app whose `visibleTo` is unset or includes any household adult id (`index.html` visibleApps and `POST /api/chat` apply that rule). `icon` is an SVG file in `icons/` (duotone: a `class="duo"` path plus stroke paths, `currentColor`), or an emoji if you must. `"dark": true` darkens the viewer while loading. Icons INSIDE an app come from the one shared sprite `icons/sprite.svg` (audit batch 4: Lucide geometry, ISC, listed in `icons/LICENSE-lucide.txt`; one weight `--icon-stroke`, sizes `--icon-*`): `<svg class="sym" aria-hidden="true"><use href="../icons/sprite.svg#i-<name>"/></svg>` beside a visible word (design.css's component half styles `svg.sym`); draw a missing glyph into the sprite in Lucide's style, never an emoji or a text glyph as an icon, and keep one drawing per meaning (04-design-system.md, Table ICON-6). The sprite is precached in `sw.js`.
4. **Give it art.** Add a spot illustration `art/app/<id>.svg` (200×160) in `scripts/make-art.mjs` and re-run it — the Home cards, empty states and the style guide pick it up from there; `scripts/test-art.mjs` fails until every app has one. See [`art/README.md`](art/README.md).
5. **Precache it** by adding the file, its icon and its art to the `SHELL` list in `sw.js`, then `node scripts/bump-sw.mjs` (bumps `VERSION` and checks the list). Skip precaching anything multi-megabyte (add it to the skip list in that script).
6. **Touch-first, all devices.** 44×44 px minimum targets (60 px+ for primary actions), no hover-only or right-click-only affordances (hover styles only under `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`), Enter/Escape on inputs and dialogs, works from ~375 px to ~1400 px wide with no horizontal scroll, `viewport-fit=cover` with safe-area insets on edge-anchored controls, `localStorage` reads/writes in `try/catch`.
7. **Commit, push, verify live:** `git add apps/<id>.html apps.json icons/<id>.svg sw.js && git commit && git push`, then poll `https://vantrix117.github.io/house-hub/apps/<id>.html` until it returns 200 and confirm `apps.json` on the live site has the entry. Report that result, not just "pushed".

Legacy `localStorage` data goes to the **first adult** who opens the app on a device; kids never receive migrated data.

## Adding a chat tool

All in [`worker/src/chat.js`](worker/src/chat.js):

1. Add the tool definition to `TOOLS` (`name`, `description`, JSON-Schema `input_schema` with `additionalProperties: false`). Keep descriptions short and concrete — the model reads them.
2. Handle it in `runTool()`: validate the input yourself, respect `profile.kind` (kids: nothing on adult-only apps; kiosk never reaches chat), write through `putOne()` from `data.js` so the normal sync rules apply, call `activity()` so it appears on Home, and return `{ ok, result, chip }`. `result` goes back to the model (keep it small); `chip` is the human line shown in the Chat tab (`"✓ Added milk to leftovers"`) — `null` for read-only tools.
3. Mention the new capability in `systemPrompt()` if the model needs a hint about when to use it.
4. Test without a key: `node scripts/mock-anthropic.mjs` + `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL="http://127.0.0.1:8791"` in `worker/.dev.vars` (git-ignored), teach the mock a trigger phrase, then `scripts/smoke-chat.sh http://127.0.0.1:8787 <local-code>`. Deploy with `npx wrangler deploy`.

## Push reminders

- Keys: `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY` and `VAPID_SUBJECT` are vars in `worker/wrangler.toml`; `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` is a secret. Regenerate the pair with `web-push`'s `generateVAPIDKeys()` if it ever leaks (everyone must re-enable notifications afterwards).
- Schedule: `[triggers] crons = ["*/15 * * * *"]` (audit batch 2b). `jobsAt()` in [`worker/src/reminders.js`](worker/src/reminders.js) reads the New York time of the firing (`event.scheduledTime`): every firing of the 8 am hour runs `morningJob` (leftovers 5+ days old → household adults) and `prayerJob`; of the 8 pm hour `behindJob` on Sundays (guests too: it is their own reading), `prayerJob` and `prayedForJob` — so a push that failed at :00 is tried again at :15, :30, :45; every firing runs `eveningJob` (no F260 reading logged today → that person, in the hour of their `push_pref:readAt`, 8 pm unless they chose another; a forced run ignores the times), `parkJob` and `prayTimeJob` (the chosen time's whole hour is eligible). The guest clean-up and the kids' 90-day chat clean-up ride on the first 8 am / 8 pm firing. Morning, evening, behind, praytime and prayedfor send at most once per person per day (delivered pushes only); prayer and park remember in `settings` (`last_prayer_push_at`, `park_alerts`) what each person has been told. Dead subscriptions are deleted; per-person toggles come from `app_data(person, hub, 'push_pref:<kind>')` over the old `push_prefs` row (`prefsFor`, `mergePrefs`).
- To add a reminder type: write a `xJob(env, now)` next to the others, add it to `JOBS` and to `jobsAt`, add a toggle in the Me tab (`renderNotif` in `index.html`) that writes the row `push_pref:x`, and log with `pushTo(env, profileId, 'x', payload)`.
- Test: `npx wrangler dev --test-scheduled` and `curl "http://127.0.0.1:8787/cdn-cgi/handler/scheduled?cron=*/15+*+*+*+*"` (what runs depends on the real clock), or force a job with `POST /api/admin/cron/run {"job":"morning"}` as the admin. `scripts/push-receiver.mjs` stands in for the push service and decrypts what the Worker sent. `scripts/test-push.mjs` checks the encryption against the RFC 8291 test vector.
