## B / A: the two calls to wire (the API)
`window.kidverseVoice` (always defined once the third script has run; test `window.kidverseVoice && ...`):
- `info(week)` -> `null` or `{ id, by, byName, at, ms, mime, label, faceHtml }`
  - `label` = "<byName>'s voice" (e.g. "Elizabeth's voice"); `faceHtml` = `hub.avatarHtml(<the recorder>, 'avatar-sm')` (their face, photo or emoji).
  - Use it to relabel the VERSE speaker when `info(week)` is non-null: face + label instead of "Read it to me" (aria-label "Elizabeth's voice: <ref>"). Re-run it from your render and on the window event `kidverse:voice`.
- `play(week, { onstart, onend }) -> Promise<boolean>`
  - fetches the recording with the session header, plays a blob URL. Resolves `true` when a recording was played (to its end, or until `stop()`); resolves `false` WITHOUT having made a sound when it cannot play (no row, offline, 404, decode error, autoplay refused). On `false`, fall back to the synthetic voice exactly as before, quietly (no toast).
  - `onstart()` fires when sound begins (set your "on"/"Reading..." state); `onend()` when it ends or is stopped (clear it). For a recording there is NO word highlight.
  - It calls `speechSynthesis.cancel()` first, and a later `speechSynthesis.speak()` by you should be preceded by `kidverseVoice.stop()` (cheap, safe to call always).
- `stop()` stops a recording that is playing (so your speaker's second tap, Escape and pagehide can call it; C also listens to Escape and pagehide itself).
- `playing()` -> the week number being played, or 0 (so "toasts are spoken" can stay quiet while a recording plays).
- `has(week)` -> boolean (same as `info(week) !== null`).
- Event: `window.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('kidverse:voice', { detail: { week } }))` whenever a `voice:` row changes (or a recording starts/stops), so labels refresh.
- Where it plays: the verse speaker (family week) and the past-weeks shelf (any week's recording; same `play(week, ...)`). Never the TV, never the story speaker.
