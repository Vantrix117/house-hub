## Summary

- **What it is.** A personal 52-week Bible reading plan: 5 readings and 2 memory verses a week. Around it sit a streak that allows 2 rest days, an encrypted HEAR journal, verse practice, milestones, pace, print, reading mode and backup. It is person scope, shown to the five household adults and to guests (`apps.json:4`), and it feeds the Home card and the 8 pm and Sunday reading nudges through `f260.summary` and `f260.log`.
- **Confirmed defects: 20** (2 critical, 0 high, 6 medium, 12 low). None were refuted or left unresolved. Eight (P3-F260-13 to -20) came from the completeness critic.
  - The two criticals:
    - **P3-F260-01:** rating a verse in F260's practice dialog erases that verse's schedule in the Verses trainer (a box-5 verse went back to box 1, due today). It needs the verse's text pasted in F260 once. The Verses report's P3-VERSES-01 is the same defect and is now a pointer to this one, so the roll-up counts it once, here.
    - **P3-F260-13 (from the critic):** a device that still has the journal unlocked writes its old ciphertext over a vault the same person erased, or erased and re-created, on another device. The journal then opens nowhere: a raw "blob.pass.salt" error, or "Wrong passcode." for both the old and the new passcode, and the entries written after starting over are lost.
  - Two mediums are about the streak:
    - **P3-F260-02:** Done then Undo leaves "Read today ✓", adds a streak day and switches off that evening's nudge.
    - **P3-F260-03:** after exactly two rest days, the streak reads 0 and "Start a new streak" until the person reads; a Monday-to-Friday reader sees this every Monday.
  - Four mediums came from the critic: the Face ID wrap is one synced slot, so other devices show a Face ID button that fails and enabling it on a second device breaks the first (P3-F260-14); the Erase and Restore confirms say "this device" but act on every device (P3-F260-15); Reset's confirm does not say it also deletes the Verses review schedule (P3-F260-16); tapping into an old HEAR entry and away re-dates it to today and adds a journal-streak day (P3-F260-17).
  - Three lows came from the critic: a week read ahead and started later reads "done in -1 days" (P3-F260-18); the − / + week stepper stamps start dates on every week it passes (P3-F260-19); journal search shows "&amp;" and "&quot;" in its results (P3-F260-20).
- **Rubric: final average 4.6 / 10** (investigator 5.1). The visual checker lowered Color, Shape, Motion, Dark mode and Ease of use by one point each. Delight is best at 6; Glanceability is weakest at 3.
- **Biggest usability gaps.**
  - The storage copy says "this device" and "this iPad", but Erase and Restore act on every device (P3-F260-15, P3-F260-12, UX-F260-1).
  - "Reset progress…" cannot be undone (UX-F260-2), and its dialog does not say it also deletes the Verses review schedule (P3-F260-16).
  - A reading missed in an earlier week is not offered again for months (GAP-F260-1).
  - A first HEAR entry takes 6 taps, a passcode and a 1,100 px scroll (UX-F260-11); the current week and Settings are thousands of pixels down (UX-F260-6, -8).
- **Biggest visual gaps.** Much of the secondary text fails AA in every palette (VIS-F260-1); empty progress cells are almost invisible, 1.04:1 in dark (VIS-F260-2, from the visual check); the person's accent never reaches the content (VIS-F260-11); emoji stand in for icons and the type is off the Dynamic Type scale (VIS-F260-12, §5).
- **What works.** The daily tick is 2 taps from Home with Done at mid-screen (OK-F260-3); the journal's encryption is sound (OK-F260-2), though its cross-device handling is not (P3-F260-13, -14); date maths survives the DST change (OK-F260-1); print gives a clean 3-page plan (OK-F260-4); kids and the kiosk cannot reach the app through the hub (a direct URL still opens it for a kid session: P2-SEC-02); there are 0 hardcoded hex colours.
- **Best-value improvement.** Merge instead of replace the `f260.recall` entry on a practice rating (delight 4, effort S), which closes P3-F260-01. Close behind, also S: make Undo un-log today, treat an unread today as pending in the streak, and guard the journal vault so a device never saves over a vault it did not unlock (closes the second critical, P3-F260-13).

### Confirmed defects

#### P3-F260-01 — Rating a verse in F260's practice dialog erases its schedule in the Verses trainer

- **Severity: critical** (skeptics: critical / critical; unchanged). Rule (a): the shipped UI silently overwrites a person's stored data (the Leitner box, due date, last review and streak of a verse) in normal use, with no dev tools.
- **Exposure.** An adult has trained a verse in Verses and later rates the same verse in F260's practice dialog, reached by tapping a memorised verse (`apps/f260.html:1648-1650`) or a "Practice again" chip (`apps/f260.html:1343-1346`). Each rating resets one verse. F260 is shown to the five adults and to guests (`apps.json:4`); Verses is also shown to the kids (`apps.json:12`), but they cannot reach F260. Only a verse whose text was pasted into F260 once (kept in `f260.verses`) shows Reveal and the ratings. Without it, the dialog shows only the paste box (`apps/f260.html:1897-1903`). The Verses report records the same precondition (`audits/03-apps/verses.md:165`). Since GAP-VERSES-1 finds text on about 1 card in 10, the precondition narrows the exposure but does not lower the severity.
- **Related.** Not a Phase 2 duplicate: `f260.recall` is not in the `audits/02-shell.md` register, and P2-SYNC-18 is a stale-map write-back, while this happens with a fresh map. Also filed from the Verses side as P3-VERSES-01. That ID is now a pointer to this one, and the roll-up counts the defect once, here.

**What happens now.** F260's "Got it" and "Not yet" both write `recall[prId] = { s, t: Date.now() }` (`apps/f260.html:1915`), a new object that never reads the previous entry, and save the whole map through `hub.set` (`apps/f260.html:902`). Verses keeps `box`, `due`, `last` and `streak` in the same entry (`apps/verses.html:294-298`) and reads a missing box as 1 and a missing due date as "due now" (`apps/verses.html:219`; its comment at `apps/verses.html:218` calls `{s, t}` rows "pre-Leitner"). Measured by the investigator on a box-4 verse:
- before: `{s:'got', box:4, due:'2026-09-29', last:'2026-09-22', streak:3}`;
- after one "Got it" in F260: `{s:'got', t:1790080807298}`;
- the Verses summary went from due 3, boxes `[2,2,6,15,40]` to due 4, boxes `[3,2,6,14,40]`.

F260 shows "★ Got it" while it does this.

**Expected.** F260 merges its fields, `recall[id] = {...recall[id], s, t}`. Better, an F260 rating advances the Leitner fields the way Verses does (`apps/verses.html:294-296`), so the two trainers share one schedule.

**Why it matters to the household.** Spaced repetition is weeks of reviews. A well-learned verse silently drops back to daily review with its streak at 0, and the F260 "Got it" is not even counted as a review in Verses.

**Evidence.**
- Code: `apps/f260.html:902, 927, 1915, 2077`; `apps/verses.html:193, 218-219, 290-298`.
- Runs: `audits/evidence/p3/f260/recall.json` (`rowBefore`, `rowAfterF260GotIt`, `versesBefore`, `versesAfter`), `audits/evidence/p3/f260/verify-recall-practice-wipes-verses-box-1.json`, `audits/evidence/p3/f260/verify-recall-practice-wipes-verses-box-2.json`.
- Screenshots: `audits/evidence/p3/f260/recall-practice-reveal-ipad.png`, `audits/evidence/p3/f260/recall-verses-after-ipad.png`, `audits/evidence/p3/f260/verify-recall-practice-wipes-verses-box-1-verses-after-ipad.png`, `audits/evidence/p3/f260/verify-recall-practice-wipes-verses-box-2-verses-after-iphone.png`.

#### P3-F260-13 — A device with the journal still unlocked overwrites a vault that another device erased or re-created; the journal then opens nowhere (from the critic)

**Expected.** Find the matches in the raw text, then escape each piece between matches and each match separately before joining them with `<mark>`, so the displayed text never changes.

**Why it matters to the household.** The result shows garbled text for the very thing just searched for.
