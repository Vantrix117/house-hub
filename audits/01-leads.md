# House Hub audit, Phase 1: leads recorded while building the capture rig

The capture agents saw these while building and reviewing the rig's screens. Each builder's and reviewer's reports were merged and deduplicated per area. They are **leads, not findings**: Phase 1 does not audit. Phases 2 and 3 must confirm each lead before it goes into `05-findings.md`.

- **Evidence.** Every lead cites evidence: a `file:line` in the baseline code (`fe6041d`) and/or a capture under `audits/screens/`.
- **Line checks.** The consolidator reopened the cited lines of the five most important code claims per area; those are marked *(line-checked)*.
- **Capture paths.** A capture path names the full-size PNG kept on disk (see `01-capture.md` → Storage). The same frame appears as a thumbnail on the screen's contact sheet in `audits/screens/_sheets/`.
